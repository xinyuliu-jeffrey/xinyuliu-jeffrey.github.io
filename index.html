I am a Research Associate at iBUG Group, Imperial College London, working with Dr. Stavros Petridis and Prof. Maja Pantic. I received my Ph.D. from CUHK, advised by Prof. Yixuan Yuan, and completed my bachelor's and master's degrees at HIT.

I was a visiting Ph.D. student at ETH Zurich with Prof. Ender Konukoglu, visited Peking University with Prof. Hongbin Han, and interned at MSRA.

My work has received the Faculty Outstanding Thesis Award, MICCAI 2022 Student Travel Award, Cell Reports Medicine 2026 Highlights, and CVPR 2022 Best Paper Finalist recognition.
